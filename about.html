About GLXTCH

👩‍💻 Who's Behind It?

I go by many names, but here — you can call me Shadowglitch.

I'm not a pro. Not yet. Just a curious mind exploring the edges of the digital world, learning how systems break — and how to understand them better.

This site is a simple project that I thought of doing.

I believe knowledge should be free, the system was never safe, and curiosity is never a crime.


Why this Project?

GLXTCH started when I first got into ethical hacking. What began as a personal project has turned into my space to experiment, learn, and share what I find. It’s a continuation of my last project, where I learned the basics of hacking. Now, I’m documenting everything here to keep track of my progress and share what I’ve learned with others who are interested.
